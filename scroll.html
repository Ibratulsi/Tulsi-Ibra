The very serious science of us.

Four important discoveries about being each other's favorite person. Please scroll responsibly; the research is extremely cute.

Discovery 01 · The quick call

“Just five minutes?” Famous last words.

One quick hello somehow becomes a full tour of the day, three new topics, and one person asking, “Wait, what were we saying?”

Call duration: scientifically “not long enough”
🫡

Discovery 02 · The meme department

A meme is worth a thousand “thinking of you”s.

Some messages deliver important updates. Others simply say, “I saw this and immediately thought of your beautiful, questionable sense of humor.” Both are essential communication.

Peer-reviewed by the group chat
👋

Discovery 03 · The goodbye loop

The call ends. Then it ends again. Then one more time.

“Okay, bye!” “Wait, one more thing.” “Now bye!” This adorable loop may continue until somebody remembers they have to press the actual button.

Estimated goodbyes: at least four
🌍

Discovery 04 · The time-zone puzzle

One “good morning” may arrive at snack o'clock.

Distance makes the clocks a little confusing, but somehow there's always time for a message, a laugh, and a reminder that you're on the same team.

The moon has entered the group chat

Study conclusion

The results are in: you're pretty great together.

Further research is recommended. Preferably involving snacks, a very long call, and absolutely no rush to say goodbye.
